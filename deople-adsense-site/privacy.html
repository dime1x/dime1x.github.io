Privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This page describes general privacy practices for deople.ai. Before launch, the site owner should review and update it to reflect the actual hosting provider, analytics tools, advertising services, contact process, and applicable legal requirements.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond to your inquiry and maintain relevant correspondence.

Log data and cookies

Our hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and timestamps in server logs for security and operational purposes. The site may use cookies or similar technologies if analytics or advertising services are enabled. Update this section to name the actual services and explain their purposes.

Advertising

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is added, that provider may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize ads, subject to the provider’s policies and your choices. Do not enable advertising until the required disclosures, consent mechanisms, and account settings are correctly configured for your visitors’ locations.

Analytics

If analytics tools are installed, describe the provider, the data collected, and how visitors can manage their choices. This starter website does not include an analytics script by default.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information through this starter website. Information may be processed by service providers that operate the website or by authorities where legally required. Retain correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose and applicable obligations.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of certain personal information. Contact us at hello@deople.ai. We may need to verify your request.

Policy updates

We may revise this policy when website features or legal requirements change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
